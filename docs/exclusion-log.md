# Exclusion Reasons (Abstract Manual Screening)

Following PRISMA 2020 guidance, records excluded at the abstract-screening
stage are reported here by grouped reason category rather than as an
individual note per record. This table supports the summary reported in
`prisma-protocol.md` and the PRISMA flow diagram.

Total records excluded: 1,339

| # | Exclusion category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No neighbourhood-scale accessibility/proximity component | Macro-, city-, or regional-scale studies (e.g. carbon emissions, land-use change, urban sprawl, compactness indices) that do not operationalise access to daily services or X-minute city components |
| 2 | Environmental/climate/thermal focus only | Urban heat island, microclimate, thermal comfort, or air-quality studies with no accessibility or proximity dimension |
| 3 | Conceptual/theoretical/discursive treatment only | Political, critical, or discourse-analytic treatments of the 15-minute city that do not define or empirically measure components |
| 4 | Compact city used as contextual label only | Studies using "compact city" descriptively (e.g. as a study-area label) without analysing it as an urban form or proximity model |
| 5 | Off-topic / unrelated discipline | Medical, engineering, materials-science, or other studies unrelated to urban form or accessibility |
| 6 | Transport/mobility without X-minute framing | Transit, travel-behaviour, or mobility studies not framed around neighbourhood-scale service access |
| 7 | Ecology/biodiversity/green space without accessibility framing | Environmental or ecological studies of green space, habitat, or vegetation without a service-access or proximity framework |
| 8 | Housing/land market studies without proximity component | Housing supply, pricing, or land-market analyses without operationalised accessibility or service-proximity measures |
